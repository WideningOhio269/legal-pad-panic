# Legal Pad Panic

A WarioWare style browser microgame drawn on a yellow legal pad. Every round you pick one of two sticky notes: a quick short game or one move in a long game.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step and no install needed. Mouse only.

## Games

Short games, one 8 second shot:
- **Maze**: hold the mouse and steer the dot to the star. 2 wall bumps and you fail.
- **Plinko**: click to drop the chip into a star slot.

Long games, one move each time they come up:
- **Tic tac toe**
- **Mancala**

## Rules in short

- 4 hearts. Failing a short game costs a heart.
- The ink pen drains 5 every time you play a short game, and 40 when you lose a long game. Winning a long game refills 30. If the ink runs dry you lose a heart and the pen refills.
- Skip a long game more than twice and the opponent moves without you.
- After pick 5 of each 10-pick lap there's a rest stage where you can draw in the sketchbook.
